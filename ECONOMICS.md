# Prune: Unit Economics

**The Value of a Lead:**
If Prune identifies $1,000/month in overspend, and Credex replaces that spend with discounted secondary-market credits taking a 10% margin, that single converted lead is worth **$100/month ($1,200 ARR)** to Credex.

**Funnel Conversion Goals:**
* Landing Page to Audit Completed: 15%
* Audit Completed to Email Capture: 20%
* Email Captured to Consultation Booked: 10%
* Consultation to Closed Deal: 30%

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):**
Using our primary GTM channel (The "Wall of Shame" on X and HN), our initial CAC is $0. If we scale to paid SaaS newsletter sponsorships (e.g., Pragmatic Engineer), I estimate a blended CAC of **$150 per closed deal**.

**The Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months:**
To reach $1,000,000 in ARR at an average contract value of $1,200/yr, we need **833 active customers**.
Working backward through the funnel:
* 833 Closed Deals requires ~2,776 Consultations.
* 2,776 Consultations requires ~27,760 captured emails.
* 27,760 emails requires ~138,800 completed audits over 18 months.

**Conclusion:** Prune must sustain roughly 250 completed audits per day to hit the $1M ARR trajectory. The viral loop (Open Graph sharing) is mathematically necessary to hit this volume without bankrupting the CAC.
