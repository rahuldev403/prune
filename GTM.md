# Go-To-Market Strategy (Prune)

## The Exact Target User
Prune is not for "startups" generally. It is specifically built for **Engineering Managers (EMs) and fractional CTOs at Series A to Series B startups (20-100 employees)**. At this stage, the company has raised capital, scaling has begun, and individual developers have aggressively expensed fragmented AI tools (Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT Plus) without centralized oversight. The EM is feeling pressure from the CFO to optimize infrastructure burn.

## The Micro-Moment (Trigger)
Right before they want this tool, the EM is staring at a consolidated Ramp or Brex dashboard at the end of the month, seeing a combined $4,000 in unstructured "Software Subscriptions" specifically tagged to OpenAI, Anthropic, and GitHub. They are wondering: *"Are we actually using all these seats, or are my devs double-dipping on Cursor and Copilot?"*

## Where They Hang Out
* **Slack Communities:** Rands Leadership Slack (specifically `#engineering-management`), CTO Craft.
* **Subreddits:** `r/ExperiencedDevs`, `r/SaaS`.
* **Newsletters/Reads:** Pragmatic Engineer, Hacker News (specifically "Ask HN: What is your current AI stack?").

## Getting the First 100 Users ($0 Budget)
We will run a "Wall of Shame" and "Wall of Fame" strategy on X (Twitter) and Hacker News. 
1. I will run 5 manual audits for friends' startups. 
2. I will post the redacted visual charts (the black and silver SpendChart) on X and HN with the hook: *"I audited 5 Series A startups' AI spend. On average, they are burning $1,200/mo on redundant Copilot licenses while already paying for Cursor. Check your stack here."*
3. Because the tool outputs a highly visual, shareable Open Graph URL, we prompt users with high savings to quote-tweet their results to "flex" how much they just saved their CFO.

## The Unfair Distribution Channel
**Open Source Boilerplates & DevTool Creators.** We don't need to reach every EM; we need to reach the people they trust. We will build a free `<script>` widget version of Prune and offer it to creators of popular SaaS boilerplates (like ShipFast or Next.js templates). They drop the widget into their dashboard as a "Check your AI overhead" feature, and we capture the leads natively from their platforms.

## Week-1 Traction Metrics (If It Works)
* **Audits Run:** 400
* **Emails Captured (Leads):** 45 (Assuming an 11% conversion rate for users showing >$100 in savings).
* **Credex Consultations Booked:** 5 highly qualified leads.
